⚠ Data Quality & Considerations

  • Negative Sales (7,356 records): Likely represent returns, cancellations, or adjustments. Consider separating into distinct categories or capping at zero for forecasting
  • Price Volatility: Slight variations may indicate promotional periods or seasonal adjustments
  • Product Portfolio: Large number of SKUs (21.8K) may lead to sparse data for individual items — consider category-level forecasting
  • Geographic Concentration: Top 10 shops drive significant volume; ensure location-specific forecasts account for unique patterns

Seasonality is the Primary Driver: Clear seasonal peaks suggest holiday shopping cycles and promotional calendars drive sales more than other factors.

Category Performance Variance: Certain product categories consistently outperform others, indicating portfolio optimization opportunities.

Location Dependency: Shop location significantly impacts sales, likely driven by foot traffic, demographics, and local competition.

Price Elasticity: Relatively stable pricing suggests price is not the primary demand driver in this market segment.
